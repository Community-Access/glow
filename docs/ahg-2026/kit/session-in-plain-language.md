# The workshop, in plain language

Monday 16 November 2026, 10:30 to 4:30 Mountain Time, at Accessing Higher
Ground in Denver.

## What it is

A hands-on day. You write a small AI helper, called an agent, for one
accessibility task from your own job. No coding. You write it in plain
English.

## Who it is for

Anyone who makes course materials accessible: disability resources,
alternate formats, instructional design, faculty, IT accessibility. You do
not need to know AI.

## What you do

1. Describe your task in six short answers.
2. Try your agent on a sample course.
3. Give it real evidence from an accessibility checker.
4. Put it in a team of agents.
5. Share it, with your name on it.
6. Plan what to try on your campus.

## What you need

A laptop and an approved AI assistant; a free GitHub account if you share.
You can use the written examples without AI and share later if approval is
pending. The setup page walks you through it:
[letitglow.app/ahg](https://letitglow.app/ahg).

## Two rules all day

Nothing private goes into any AI. A person checks everything an agent
writes.
