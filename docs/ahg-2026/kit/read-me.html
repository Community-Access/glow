Welcome to the AHG 2026 agent kit

You are in the right place. This page opens by itself when you open the kit in VS Code. Nothing here needs code, and nothing here can break.

Three paths into the same day

Every path gives you the same agent, the same team report and the same share on GitHub. letitglow.app/ahg/paths compares them.

  • In a browser: no kit needed. Open letitglow.app/ahg/assistant, copy each block's text, and paste it into ChatGPT, Copilot on github.com, Claude, Gemini or your campus assistant. The same texts are in this kit, in any-assistant/.
  • In VS Code on your computer: you are probably reading this there. Each block is one command in Copilot Chat.
  • In VS Code for the Web: the same, at vscode.dev in Chrome or Edge, with this folder opened from your laptop.

Start here, in VS Code

  1. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac.
  2. Type /ready-check and press Enter. Copilot checks five things and tells you that you are ready.

If a banner says Restricted Mode, choose Manage, then Trust first. It is the workshop kit, and Copilot cannot work on its files until you trust it.

Every block of the day has its own command, listed below, and its own step card in the step-cards folder.

You cannot fall behind

Every step has a finished version in examples/. If anything goes wrong, start from one of the six ready-made agents in examples/agents/, put your name on it, and carry on. That is still your agent, and it still goes into the open-source project with your name on it. If VS Code gives you trouble, switch to the browser path and carry on from the same block.

The two rules

  1. Never paste anything private into any AI: student records, health or disability information, or anyone's name. Use the sample course.
  2. Every agent ends with a person checking its work.
