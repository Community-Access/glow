# Step card 6: Share it

Block 6 of 7, 3:05 to 3:40.

## Where we are

Step 6 of 7: share your best version if approved, or keep it for later.

## What you will have at the end

If approved, your agent in the AHG 2026 collection on GitHub and a reply that
links to it. Otherwise, a reviewed local copy and a specific next step for
seeking permission and sharing later.

## Why and where

Sharing lets others use your work; it is a separate choice from saving. Use the
browser share page for every path. Opening the GitHub form sends the agent text
to GitHub before you submit it, so check permission first.

## Steps

The same on every path. If you shared a practice agent before the day, these
steps will be familiar.

Before uploading, check that you have permission to publish the instructions
under the MIT licence. Remove internal procedures, private data and non-public
links. The share form cannot detect every disclosure. If approval is pending,
keep the file locally and arrange later sharing with a helper.

1. In your browser, open letitglow.app/ahg/share.
2. Leave "My real agent" chosen.
3. Give the page your agent. In VS Code: choose Your agent file, and pick
   my-agent/SKILL.md after checking that it is version 4. In a browser: choose
   agent-v4.md, or paste your complete reviewed agent into "Your agent, as
   text". The page fills in your agent's short name, and says so.
4. Press "Open the share form on GitHub". A new tab opens with GitHub's Share my
   agent form, already filled in: the workshop code, "My real agent", and your
   agent.
5. Move to the end of the form. Select the check box that says your agent
   contains nothing private.
6. Press "Submit new issue".
7. Wait about a minute, then read the reply that appears on the page. Press F5
   to refresh if your screen reader does not announce it.

If sharing is not approved or GitHub is unavailable, save your reviewed agent
and record who can confirm permission and when you will return to sharing. This
is a deferred contribution, not a completed public share.

## You are on track if

If shared, the reply begins: Your agent is in. It names your agent, gives it a
number in the collection, and links to it. A submitted form alone is not
confirmation. If sharing is deferred, your reviewed local file and permission
follow-up are recorded.

## If you are stuck

If the reply begins Nearly there, it lists exactly what to fix. Open the menu at
the top of the issue, choose Edit, fix it, and save; it is checked again
straight away. If GitHub asks you to sign in, sign in and press the button
again. Anything else: raise a hand, and a helper shares it with you. An agent
shared with help is still yours.

Raise a hand at any time. A helper comes to you.

## Your win

You have either contributed an approved agent with your chosen credit or kept a
reviewed copy for later. Next, use what you learned to draft a 30-day campus
plan at 3:40. Keep the confirmation link or deferred-sharing owner and date in
your workshop notes.

## On Monday

If shared, your agent stays in the collection at
github.com/Community-Access/ahg-2026 for others to use and improve. Otherwise
follow up on the permission and sharing step you recorded.
