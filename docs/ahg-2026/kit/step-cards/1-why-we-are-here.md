# Step card 1: Why we are here

Block 1 of 7, 10:30 to 10:50.

## Where we are

Step 1 of 7: the problem. A course full of barriers, and a team too small.

## What you will have at the end

A picture of where the day ends: a team report on a whole course, with one section written by an agent you build.

## Steps

1. Listen. Nothing to type yet.

### In a browser

1. Open letitglow.app/ahg/assistant in one tab, and your AI assistant, signed in, in another.
2. Start a new conversation. Use this one conversation all day.
3. Under "Block 1: the ready check", press the Copy button.
4. Switch to your assistant's tab with Control+Tab, move to the message box, paste with Control+V on Windows or Command+V on a Mac, and press Enter.

### In VS Code

1. Open the kit if it is not open already: File, then Open Folder, then the ahg-2026-agent-kit folder. In VS Code for the Web, choose Open Folder at vscode.dev in Chrome or Edge. The welcome page opens.
2. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac.
3. Type /ready-check and press Enter.

## You are on track if

Your assistant or Copilot ends the ready check with: You are ready for 16 November.

## If you are stuck

If the ready check says something needs a hand, a helper fixes it with you while the room carries on. If VS Code will not cooperate, switch to the browser path: it needs nothing installed, and you will not miss anything.

Raise a hand at any time. A helper comes to you.

## Your win

You are set up. That was the hardest part of the day, and it is done.

## On Monday

The two rules travel with you: nothing private goes into any AI, and a person reviews everything an agent writes.
