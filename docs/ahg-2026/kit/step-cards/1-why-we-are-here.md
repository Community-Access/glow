# Step card 1: Why we are here

Block 1 of 7, 10:30 to 11:05 Mountain Time, Monday 16 November, in Matchless
at the Hilton Denver City Center. Next: block 2, Design your agent, at 11:05.

## Where we are

Step 1 of 7: the problem. A course full of barriers, and a team too small.
This block gets every laptop ready and shows where the day ends, so the next
six blocks make sense.

## What you will have at the end

A picture of where the day ends: a team report on a whole course, with one
section written by an agent you build.

A laptop that is ready, a map of the kit and the website, and the four parts of
an agent: your instructions, the AI model, the evidence it cites, and the person
who checks its work.

## Steps

1. Listen. Nothing to type yet.

### In a browser

1. Open [letitglow.app/ahg/assistant](https://letitglow.app/ahg/assistant) in one tab, and your AI assistant, signed
   in, in another.
2. Start a new conversation. Use this one conversation all day.
3. Under "Block 1: the ready check", press the Copy button.
4. Switch to your assistant's tab. Control+Tab moves through tabs; repeat it
   until you reach your assistant, or choose its tab by name. Move to the
   message box, paste with Control+V on Windows or Command+V on a Mac, and press
   Enter.
5. Follow the save/reopen check: copy the template to "Your agent to save" on
   the workshop page, choose "Practice template" under "Version to save", then
   "Download this version". Reopen practice-SKILL.md and confirm its seven
   headings in your assistant. Sharing is not required for readiness.

### In VS Code

1. Open the kit if it is not open already: File, then Open Folder, then the
   ahg-2026-agent-kit folder. In VS Code for the Web, choose Open Folder at
   vscode.dev in Chrome or Edge. The welcome page opens.
2. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac.
3. Type /ready-check and press Enter.
4. Allow the expected practice-file write in my-agent/history, reopen the file
   and confirm its headings. Read checks alone do not prove saving works.

## You are on track if

Your assistant or Copilot ends the ready check with: You are ready for 16
November. You have also saved, found and reopened the practice file yourself.

## If you are stuck

If the ready check says something needs a hand, a helper fixes it with you while
the room carries on. If VS Code will not cooperate, use an approved browser
assistant or a written example with a helper.

Raise a hand at any time. A helper comes to you.

## Your win

You are set up. Next, in block 2, describe one task and make your first agent at
11:05. Bring your chosen role and one non-sensitive problem.

## Back at work

The two rules travel with you: nothing private goes into any AI, and a person
reviews everything an agent writes.
