# Step card 3: Your agent at work

Block 3 of 7, 11:50 to 12:15.

## Where we are

Step 3 of 7: your agent at work on one course document.

## What you will have at the end

Your agent's first answer on a real course document, and a sense of where it had to guess.

## Steps

1. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Command+Control+I on a Mac.
2. Type /try-my-agent and press Enter.
3. Choose a file from the PSY 101 sample course. Copilot suggests the one that best fits your agent.
4. Read your agent's answer. Then read Copilot's short note: one thing it did well, and where it guessed.

## You are on track if

You have an answer, and you can name one thing your agent had to guess.

## If you are stuck

Use a ready-made agent from examples/agents for this step, or read the first answer in examples/maria-alternate-format-planner.md, section 4.

Raise a hand at any time. A helper comes to you.

## Your win

Your agent has done real work on a real course document. The guessing is the setup for after lunch.

## On Monday

Try your agent on one document from your own work, with nothing private in it.
