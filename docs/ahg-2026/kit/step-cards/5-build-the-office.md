# Step card 5: Build the office

Block 5 of 7, 2:15 to 3:05.

## Where we are

Step 5 of 7: your agent in the team, across the whole course.

## What you will have at the end

Your agent working inside a team of agents across the whole PSY 101 course, one team report with your agent's section in it, and version 4 of your agent: the best one, which you share next.

## Steps

### In a browser

1. In the same conversation, paste the text for "Block 5: build the office" from letitglow.app/ahg/assistant, and press Enter. It is the longest text of the day: the coordinator, eight specialists and every checker report.
2. Your assistant says what is about to happen, then works through the course one file at a time, saying which specialists worked on each file.
3. Read the team report. Find the section your agent wrote.
4. Read the last line: what your agent added to the team's work.

### In VS Code

1. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac.
2. Type /run-the-office and press Enter.
3. Copilot tells you what is about to happen, then works through the course one file at a time, saying which specialists worked on each file. Screen reader users hear each file as it finishes.
4. Read the team report. Find the section your agent wrote. Alt+F2 on Windows, or Option+F2 on a Mac, shows the whole report as plain text.
5. Read the last line: what your agent added to the team's work.

### Then make the next version, in a browser

1. In the same conversation, paste the review text from letitglow.app/ahg/assistant, under "Review your agent", and press Enter.
2. Read the twelve Yes or Not yet lines, then the one change it suggests and why.
3. Say yes, or reword the change in your own words. Your assistant gives you version 4 of your agent in a box with a Copy button. Copy it and keep it, the same way as in block 2.

### Then make the next version, in VS Code

1. Type /review-my-agent and press Enter.
2. Read the twelve Yes or Not yet lines, then the one change it suggests and why.
3. Say yes, or reword the change. Copilot saves version 4 of your agent and installs it.

## You are on track if

The report names your agent and you as its author, in at least one file's section, and you have version 4.

## If you are stuck

If the run stops, type: continue. If your assistant has forgotten your agent, paste it again. At the end of this block, watch the projector: the full Accessibility Agents project runs on the same course.

Raise a hand at any time. A helper comes to you.

## Your win

You built part of an agent team today, and it just worked through a whole course.

## On Monday
