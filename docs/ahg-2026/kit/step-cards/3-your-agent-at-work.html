Step card 3: Your agent at work

Block 3 of 7, 11:45 to 12:15 Mountain Time. Before this: block 2, Design your agent. Next: lunch, 12:15 to 1:15, then block 4, Ground it, at 1:15.

Where we are

Step 3 of 7: your agent at work on one course document.

What you will have at the end

Your agent's first answer on a real course document, and version 2 of your agent, revised from what that answer showed.

Why and where

This first try shows what the agent can and cannot say without a checker report. Browser participants stay in the same assistant conversation and use the workshop page for prompts and local saving. Both VS Code paths use Copilot Chat and the open kit.

Steps

In a browser

  1. In the same conversation as block 2, paste the text for "Block 3: your agent at work" from letitglow.app/ahg/assistant, and press Enter.
  2. Your assistant uses your agent from block 2. If it asks for your agent, paste it, or open your downloaded SKILL.md and copy it from there.
  3. Choose a file from the PSY 101 sample course. Your assistant suggests the one that best fits your agent.
  4. Read your agent's answer. Then read the short note: one thing it did well, and whether it guessed or asked for missing evidence.
  5. Copy the complete first answer and chosen course filename into a local workshop-notes document. Save it before reviewing the agent.

In VS Code

  1. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac.
  2. Type /try-my-agent and press Enter.
  3. Choose a file from the PSY 101 sample course. Copilot suggests the one that best fits your agent.
  4. Read your agent's answer. Then read Copilot's short note: one thing it did well, and whether it guessed or asked for missing evidence. To read a long answer as plain text, press Alt+F2 on Windows or Option+F2 on a Mac.
  5. Approve saving the answer and filename in my-agent/workshop-notes.md, then reopen the file to check it. If writing is unavailable, copy them to an approved local document instead.

Then make the next version, in a browser

  1. Return to letitglow.app/ahg/assistant. Under block 3, press "Copy the review text for block 3", then paste it in the same assistant conversation and press Enter.
  2. Read the twelve Yes or Not yet lines, then the one change it suggests and why.
  3. Say yes, or reword the change in your own words. Your assistant gives you version 2 of your agent in a box with a Copy button.
  4. Copy the whole agent to "Your agent to save" on the workshop page. Choose version 2 under "Version to save" and press "Download this version". Reopen agent-v2.md and check its version and complete content. Keep agent-v1.md too; never approve replacing an earlier copy.

Then make the next version, in VS Code

  1. Type /review-my-agent and press Enter.
  2. Read the twelve Yes or Not yet lines, then the one change it suggests and why.
  3. Say yes, or reword the change. Copilot saves version 2 of your agent and installs it.
  4. Reopen my-agent/history/agent-v1.md and agent-v2.md. Both must still contain their own complete version; the working copy is version 2.

You are on track if

You have an answer, you can identify a guess or an appropriate request for evidence, and you have version 2. Asking for the missing report is a success; do not make the agent invent findings to finish this block.

If you are stuck

If the assistant forgets your agent, provide its latest saved version, current block, chosen course filename and saved first answer, then repeat the block prompt. If copying is blocked, follow the selected-text instruction and copy manually. The day-at-a-glance guide has the complete written-example route if AI is unavailable.

Raise a hand at any time. A helper comes to you.

Your win

You tested how your agent handles missing evidence, and made its instructions clearer. Keep the answer and version 2. After lunch, use the same file's report in block 4 and verify the new answer.

Before you leave for lunch at 12:15, answer two questions, with no name, at letitglow.app/ahg/check-in. It tells the helpers who to see first. Lunch ends at 1:15; come back to the same room.
