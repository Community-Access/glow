Step card 0: Before the day

About 10 to 25 minutes, any time before Monday 16 November, depending on your path. Everything starts at letitglow.app/ahg.

The workshop runs 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM Mountain Time, in Matchless at the Hilton Denver City Center, in seven blocks. This card is the setup before block 1. On the day, block 1 repeats the ready check at 10:30, and the setup table outside the room opens at 10:00.

What you will have at the end

A laptop that is ready for the workshop and a message from your AI assistant saying so. If public sharing is approved, you will also have shared a practice agent. Otherwise keep it local and note how to seek approval.

New to words like agent, skill or Copilot Chat? Read glossary.md in the kit, or the glossary online, first. It is one page.

Choose your path

The day is the same on every path. Not sure? Take the browser path. letitglow.app/ahg/paths compares them. Choose an approved path now so you can stay in it during the exercises. Your day at a glance, also in the kit, shows where each block leads.

  • The browser path: nothing to install. ChatGPT, Copilot on github.com, Claude, Gemini or your campus assistant, in any browser.
  • VS Code on your computer: install VS Code and type one Copilot command per block.
  • VS Code for the Web: the same VS Code and Copilot at vscode.dev, in Chrome or Edge, with nothing to install.

Steps

Use only tools and accounts your campus permits. Turning off model training does not authorize uploading private data. No approved AI or sharing option? Work through the written examples with a helper in Word or on paper and keep your agent local until approval is confirmed.

Everyone

  1. If your chosen path or approved sharing needs GitHub, make a free account at github.com, if you do not have one. Use your work email if your institution allows it. If GitHub asks you to set up two-factor sign-in, choose the option that works best with your screen reader or phone; GitHub Mobile and an authenticator app both work.
  2. Answer the five-minute survey at letitglow.app/ahg/survey: your name and registration email, your path, what your laptop and campus allow, and anything we should have ready for you. A GitHub username is optional.

In a browser

  1. Open your assistant in a new tab and sign in: chatgpt.com, github.com/copilot, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, or m365copilot.com with your work account. Sign in even if the assistant lets you start without it: ChatGPT stops after a message or two without an account.
  2. Optional, and a good habit: turn off training. In ChatGPT, open your profile menu, then Settings, then Data controls, and turn off "Improve the model for everyone". letitglow.app/ahg/paths has the steps for the others.
  3. In another tab, open letitglow.app/ahg/assistant. Under "Block 1: the ready check", press the Copy button. A message under the button says it is copied.
  4. Switch to your assistant's tab. Control+Tab moves through tabs; repeat it until you reach your assistant, or choose its tab by name. Move to the message box, paste with Control+V on Windows or Command+V on a Mac, and press Enter. Complete its save/reopen check before optional sharing: copy the template to "Your agent to save", choose "Practice template" under "Version to save", press "Download this version", then reopen practice-SKILL.md and confirm its seven headings in the assistant.
  5. If public sharing is approved, share your practice agent. Open letitglow.app/ahg/share, choose "My practice agent", press "Fill in the practice template", and press "Open the share form on GitHub". On GitHub, sign in if asked, select the privacy check box at the end of the form, and press "Submit new issue". If sharing is not approved, keep the saved practice file locally and record who can confirm permission.

In VS Code on your computer

  1. Install VS Code from code.visualstudio.com. On Windows it installs without administrator rights. You need version 1.143 or later.
  2. Open VS Code. Open the Accounts menu, at the bottom of the bar on the left, and sign in with GitHub. Copilot Free turns on by itself for an account with no Copilot plan.
  3. Import the AHG 2026 profile: open File, then Preferences, then Profiles. Open the menu on the New Profile button, choose Import Profile, paste the profile address, and choose Create. Copy the address from the AHG 2026 page, letitglow.app/ahg, where it is printed in full under "For both VS Code paths"; it ends in ahg-2026.code-profile. It makes the text larger and turns on screen reader support. If pasting the address does not work, download the AHG 2026 profile file, choose Select File instead of pasting, pick the downloaded file, and choose Create.
  4. Download the agent kit and unzip it. Open the folder in VS Code: File, then Open Folder.
  5. If a banner says Restricted Mode, choose Manage, then Trust. It is the workshop kit, and Copilot cannot work on its files until you trust it.
  6. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac. Type /ready-check and press Enter. It asks to save a practice copy in my-agent/history. Approve only that expected write, then reopen the file and confirm its seven headings. If saving fails, setup needs a hand.
  7. If public sharing is approved, share your practice agent. Open letitglow.app/ahg/share in your browser, choose "My practice agent", choose the file my-agent/SKILL.md from the kit, and press "Open the share form on GitHub". On GitHub, select the privacy check box at the end of the form and press "Submit new issue".

In VS Code for the Web

  1. Download the agent kit and unzip it. On Windows, open the zip and choose Extract all. On a Mac, open the zip.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, go to vscode.dev. Safari and Firefox cannot open a folder from your laptop.
  3. Choose Open Folder, and pick the ahg-2026-agent-kit folder. Your browser asks whether vscode.dev may see the files, and later whether it may save changes. Allow both.
  4. Open the Accounts menu, at the bottom of the bar on the left, and sign in with GitHub.
  5. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac. Type /ready-check and press Enter. Check that the command is found and can save the practice copy, then reopen it. If commands or file writes are unavailable, ask a helper for an approved browser or written route.
  6. If public sharing is approved, share your practice agent, as in step 7 of the VS Code steps above.

You are on track if

Your assistant or Copilot ends the ready check with: You are ready for 16 November after you have saved and reopened the practice file. If you shared a practice agent, GitHub replies: Well done. If sharing is not approved, you have kept it locally and identified who can confirm approval.

If you are stuck

Write to jeffbis@arizona.edu before the day, or come to the setup table outside the room from 10:00 on the day. Locked-down laptop, or VS Code blocked at work? Take the browser path; it needs nothing installed. GitHub blocked at work? Write to us; nobody is left out. Use only an approved alternative. The written-example route in your-day-at-a-glance.md gives all seven steps without AI or an account.

Your win

You are set up, and your practice is either shared or safely kept for later. Bring your chosen path and this card to block 1, Why we are here, at 10:30 on Monday 16 November.
