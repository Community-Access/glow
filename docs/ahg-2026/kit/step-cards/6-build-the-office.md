# Step card 6: Build the office

Block 6 of 7, 3:00 to 3:45.

## Where we are

Step 6 of 7: your agent in the team, across the whole course.

## What you will have at the end

Your agent working inside a team of agents across the whole PSY 101 course, and one team report with your agent's section in it.

## Steps

### In a browser

1. In the same conversation, paste the text for "Block 6: build the office" from letitglow.app/ahg/assistant, and press Enter. It is the longest text of the day: the coordinator, eight specialists and every checker report.
2. Your assistant says what is about to happen, then works through the course one file at a time, saying which specialists worked on each file.
3. Read the team report. Find the section your agent wrote.
4. Read the last line: what your agent added to the team's work.

### In VS Code

1. Open Copilot Chat: Control+Alt+I on Windows, or Control+Command+I on a Mac.
2. Type /run-the-office and press Enter.
3. Copilot tells you what is about to happen, then works through the course one file at a time, saying which specialists worked on each file. Screen reader users hear each file as it finishes.
4. Read the team report. Find the section your agent wrote. Alt+F2 on Windows, or Option+F2 on a Mac, shows the whole report as plain text.
5. Read the last line: what your agent added to the team's work.

## You are on track if

The report names your agent and you as its author, in at least one file's section.

## If you are stuck

If the run stops, type: continue. If your assistant has forgotten your agent, paste it again. Watch the projector run too: every agent the room shared runs together at the end of this block.

Raise a hand at any time. A helper comes to you.

## Your win

You built part of an agent team today, and it just worked through a whole course.
