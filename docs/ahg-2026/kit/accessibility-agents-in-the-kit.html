Accessibility Agents in this kit

This kit carries all 113 Accessibility Agents skills, from commit 2f4fe22 of Accessibility Agents, in .github/skills. Open the kit in VS Code, on a computer or at vscode.dev, and they are there: start a new chat and ask for one by name. Nothing to install.

They are under the project's MIT licence, which is in accessibility-agents-LICENSE.txt in this kit.

The kit cannot carry the project's scanner tools, its scripts or its hooks. So every skill below works, but some work differently here. The full version, with everything, is one click away in Codespaces: accessibility-agents.md in this kit says how.
