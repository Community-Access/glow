# Pre-event message

Send this notice to registered participants today, 9 November. Send a short
reminder on 14 November. It is the setup checklist for the day, so it has to
be short, numbered and impossible to misread.

Keep the first screen to what to do. Everything else comes after.

---

## Subject

Accessibility Agents workshop, 16 November: choose a path, 10 to 25 minutes

## Body

Hello,

You are booked into Accessibility Agents: Building Human-Centered AI
Workflows, the full-day workshop at Accessing Higher Ground on Monday 16
November, 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM Mountain Time, in Matchless.

By the end of the day you will have designed your own accessibility agent,
added it to a team of agents that works through a whole course, and
contributed it to an open-source project with your name on it. You do not
need to be a developer. You do not need to have used VS Code or GitHub
before. Every step on the day comes with a step card and a helper.

### Before the day: choose a path

There are three paths into the same day, and every one ends with your own
agent, shared on GitHub with your name on it. [Compare the three paths](https://letitglow.app/ahg/paths).
Not sure? Take the browser path.

- The browser path, about 10 minutes, nothing to install: use ChatGPT,
  Copilot on github.com, Claude, Gemini or your campus assistant, signed in.
  No public directory listing or connector setup is needed.
- VS Code on your computer, about 25 minutes: install VS Code and import the
  AHG 2026 profile.
- VS Code for the Web, about 15 minutes, nothing to install: the same VS
  Code at vscode.dev, in Chrome or Edge.

Then, on any path:

1. Make a free GitHub account at github.com, if you do not have one. Use your
   work email if your institution allows it.
2. Follow your path's steps on [the setup page](https://letitglow.app/ahg/paths),
   and run the ready check. It takes two minutes and tells you whether you
   are set.
3. Share your practice agent with [the practice-agent form](https://letitglow.app/ahg/share?practice=1):
   one form, one button, and a reply within a minute. On the day you do it
   once more, with your real agent.
4. Answer [the five-minute survey](https://letitglow.app/ahg/survey). It asks
   for your name and registration email, your role and setup, any access
   needs, and what you hope to work on. A GitHub username is optional. The
   workshop team uses your answers to prepare; they stay in GLOW, are not
   sent to an AI or GitHub, and will be deleted by 31 January 2027. Do not
   include student names, records or accommodation details.

Want the short version to pass on to a colleague? Read the
[plain-language workshop description](https://letitglow.app/ahg/kit/session-in-plain-language.md).

If a step does not work, [write to the workshop support team](mailto:support@community-access.org)
before the day, or come to the setup table outside the room from 10:00 on
the day.
Locked-down work laptop? Take the browser path: it needs nothing
installed. Or write to us; we will sort it out together, and nobody is left
out on the day.

Use only an assistant your campus permits. A browser does not bypass campus
data or account rules. If no AI tool or public sharing is approved, bring
the written examples and work in Word or on paper with a helper; keep your
agent local until sharing is approved.

### Bring

- A Windows or Mac laptop and its charger.
- Headphones, if you use a screen reader. Read "If you use a screen
  reader" in the [kit README](https://letitglow.app/ahg/kit) before the day.
- One real accessibility problem from your job. Nothing private: no student
  names, records or accommodation details. You will work on a sample course,
  and your problem is what makes your agent yours.

### What the day looks like

- Morning: design your agent in plain English, and see it work on a course
  document with GitHub Copilot or the assistant you chose.
- After lunch: ground it in real evidence from GLOW, axe and Accessibility
  Insights, so you can verify its claims and WCAG 2.2 citations.
- Afternoon: add it to an agent team and watch the team work through the
  whole course, then share it to the open-source AHG 2026 collection on
  GitHub, with one form and your name on it.
- The last five minutes are the session evaluation. It shapes next year's
  conference.

### If you need anything

Large print, a particular seat, a pause, a repeat: ask for any of it, before
or during. That is not an interruption of this workshop; it is the point of
it.

See you in Denver,

Jeff Bishop
[University of Arizona](https://accessibility.arizona.edu), IT Accessibility Consultant

In collaboration with [Blind Information Technology Solutions (BITS)](https://www.bits-acb.org) and [Techopolis](https://www.techopolis.app), with special thanks to Taylor Arndt, who helped dream up and build Accessibility Agents.
