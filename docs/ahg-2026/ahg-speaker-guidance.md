# AHG 2026 speaker guidance, checked against this workshop

The conference sent four documents to speakers on 9 October 2026: the speaker
orientation outline, the guided notes, the "Speaker Best Practices" slides,
and the Cvent Speaker Resource Center instructions. This is every
recommendation in them, where the workshop meets it, and what only a person
can still do. Checked against the redesigned day on 9 October 2026.

## Accessible, in the digital sense

Each row is one recommendation from the orientation.

| Recommendation | Where it is met |
|---|---|
| Materials meet WCAG 2 | The deck in Word and PowerPoint and every step card pass GLOW's own audit with no critical, high or medium findings, and tests hold the deck to that. The projected deck is in the axe sweep. Participant Markdown has no bold or italic emphasis |
| Use a checker | GLOW is the checker, and the day teaches participants to use one: block 4 grounds every agent in GLOW, axe-core and Accessibility Insights evidence |
| Complete manual checks | The screen reader walkthrough in `run-of-show.md` pre-flight item 6, with NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver, still to do |
| Fix any issues | Fixed on 9 October: Word headings and fonts, PowerPoint text under 18pt and stray bullets, small and all-caps text in the projected deck, and a 12pt printout |

## Accessible, in the design sense

Each row is one recommendation from the orientation, and where the workshop meets it.

| Recommendation | Where it is met |
|---|---|
| Avoid walls of text | A test fails if any slide has more than seven bullets or a table of more than nine rows. Detail lives in speaker notes and step cards |
| Chunking | Seven blocks, each with one outcome, one command and one step card |
| Practice demos; dry run live activities | Every block starts with a live demo; pre-flight items 6 to 8 are the walkthrough, the allowance check and a timed dry run |
| Have a backup plan | The fallback ladder in `run-of-show.md`, and a ready-made agent in the kit for every role card |

## Accessible, in the delivery sense

Each row is one recommendation from the orientation, and where the workshop meets it.

| Recommendation | Where it is met |
|---|---|
| Wear the mic | Slide 1 speaker notes, the run of show and the pocket card |
| Slow down, the two-thirds rule | Every block is mostly participants doing; the run of show says talk for a third |
| Describe visuals | Speaker notes on every table slide say to read it aloud; the coordinator announces each file as it finishes, so screen reader users hear progress |
| Key point and why care, up front | Slide 1 notes and slide 2: forty thousand files and three people |

## Practical and engaging

Each row is one recommendation from the orientation, and where the workshop meets it.

| Recommendation | Where it is met |
|---|---|
| More hands-on | Six of the seven blocks are hands-on, each with its own Copilot command |
| Leave with a skill, a tool or a framework | An agent, a team report, a pull request and a 30-day plan, and the five-part agent formula |
| Fix pacing | One agenda module drives the deck and the run of show; the pacing rules say what to protect |
| Title and content match | The deck's title is the program's title, word for word, and a test holds it there. Every promise in the session description is mapped to a block in `plan.md` section 5 |
| Skilled demos | Every block opens with "watch me first" on the projector, with a written example to fall back on |
| Stories | Maria, Jordan and Sam, colleagues at a fictional university, each one step ahead of the room |

## A word on AI

Each row is one recommendation from the orientation, and where the workshop meets it.

| Recommendation | Where it is met |
|---|---|
| Say what you will and will not cover | Slide 6 speaker notes |
| Address ethical and responsible use | Slide 6: never paste anything private, and a named person reviews every agent. The same rules are built into every agent's "Never" section and into the kit's instructions to Copilot |
| Do not alienate the hesitant, or the advocates | Slide 6 notes name privacy, accuracy and jobs once, and answer each |
| Say how this differs from the other AI sessions | Slide 6 notes: higher education accessibility work at scale, built by the people who do it, open source |
| Keep the emphasis on accessibility | Every agent and every example is about a real student barrier |

## Logistics

Each row is one recommendation from the orientation, and where the workshop meets it.

| Recommendation | Where it is met |
|---|---|
| Save time for the session evaluation | The last five minutes of the AHG day in the agenda module; slides 5 and 26 |
| Bring your own laptop, charger and adaptors | The pocket card checklist |
| Visit the room ahead of time | The pocket card checklist |

| Times are Mountain Time | The setup message, the setup page, the run of show, the pocket card and slide 5 |
| Upload materials to Cvent (DOC, DOCX, PPTX, PDF; under 250 MB) | Run of show pre-flight item 5: `slides.pptx`, `slides.docx` and the step cards |

## Only a person can do these

1. Upload the deck and step cards to Cvent, and check the session details
   there.
2. Update the Cvent profile: photo, bio and email.
3. Fill in the pocket card: proctor, AV contact, wifi, projector input.
4. Run the screen reader walkthrough and the timed dry run.
5. Ask AHG to change "2-day" to one day on the session page.
