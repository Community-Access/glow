This is the browser path: the whole day in a web browser, with the AI assistant you already have. Every block has one text. You copy it, paste it into your assistant as one message, and answer its questions. You work toward the same agent, team report and plan as the VS Code paths. Keep this page and your assistant open; the share page comes in block 6.

You can try ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot on github.com, Claude, Gemini or Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, including the one your campus provides. Responses and message limits vary; use a campus-approved assistant. Setting up your assistant has the steps for each, and how to stop your chats being used for training.

Before you start

  1. Open your assistant in a second browser tab, and sign in. Signing in matters: ChatGPT without an account stops after a message or two and asks you to log in.
  2. Start one new conversation, and use that same conversation all day. Your agent from block 2 stays in it, so the later blocks can find it.
  3. Never paste anything private: no student names, records, accommodation or health details. We use a fictional course all day.
  4. Keep your day at a glance available. It includes the saved outputs, recovery steps and a written-example route if AI is unavailable or not approved.

How every block works

  1. On this page, press the block's Copy button. A message under the button says the text is copied.
  2. Switch to your assistant's tab. Control+Tab moves through tabs on Windows or Mac; repeat it until you reach your assistant, or choose its tab by name.
  3. Move to the message box, where you would type a question. Paste with Control+V on Windows, or Command+V on a Mac.
  4. Press Enter to send. The assistant starts the block, usually by asking you one question.
  5. Answer each question in the same message box. When it gives you something to keep, it puts it in a box with its own Copy button.

Want to read a text before you paste it? Each block has "Read the text first". The evidence-based blocks carry the relevant kit files. Check the assistant's claims against those sources; evidence reduces guessing but does not guarantee a correct answer.

Keep a copy of your agent

After block 2 and each review in blocks 3, 4 and 5, copy the complete agent from your assistant and paste it here. Download a copy before you continue. Saving here does not send your agent to GLOW or GitHub. Choose the version that matches the agent's version line. The filenames are practice-SKILL.md and agent-v1.md through agent-v4.md. Keep each version and choose agent-v4.md when you share after the final review. Never replace an earlier file: if asked to replace it, choose Cancel and save the new version with a different name or in a different folder. If downloading is unavailable, paste into a plain-text editor and save with the same versioned name. Reopen each download to check its complete text and version. This page does not retain your pasted text after you close or reload it.

Keep a local workshop-notes document too: record your current block, chosen course filename, first answer, grounded comparison and team report. Save the 30-day plan separately. These outputs let you resume after a lost conversation.

If something goes wrong

  • The assistant asks you to log in: sign in or make a free account, then paste the block's text again.
  • The assistant says the message is too long: a new conversation may not change its per-message limit. Keep your saved work and use the written-example route in the day-at-a-glance guide, or a tested campus-approved assistant with a helper. Do not remove evidence to make the prompt fit.
  • Before changing conversations or tools, save your agent and workshop notes. In the new conversation, paste the latest agent, then say your current block and chosen course filename. Paste the previous answer from your notes before requesting a before-and-after comparison, then paste the block text. If that answer is lost, label the comparison incomplete; do not reconstruct it as an actual result.
  • The assistant has used up its free messages for now: use another campus-approved assistant or a written example with a helper, and carry on from this block. Your agent is on your computer, from block 2.
  • Anything else: raise a hand, or write to jeffbis@arizona.edu.
