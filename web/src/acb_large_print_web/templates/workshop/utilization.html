Use this guide to run Workshop Mode as a repeatable institutional experience with measurable learning outcomes.

Pre-event readiness

  • Enable workshop feature flags and validate route access.
  • Run keyboard-only and screen-reader smoke tests.
  • Prepare backup worksheets for offline fallback.
  • Confirm facilitator script, timing, and support contacts.

During workshop delivery

  • Keep framing on partner confidence and ownership.
  • Require human-review checkpoints in every AI-assisted artifact.
  • Use the gallery and peer feedback to strengthen trust and reuse.
  • Capture capstone artifacts and 30-day action commitments.

Post-event follow through

  • Export artifacts in Markdown for sharing and versioning.
  • Package top workflows into prompts and checklists for local teams.
  • Run a 30-day follow-up to measure adoption and confidence gains.
  • Promote successful workflows into reusable GLOW skills.

Utilization metrics

  • Activity completion rates
  • Capstone workflow quality and safeguard coverage
  • Artifact reuse at 30 days
  • Participant confidence delta, before and after
