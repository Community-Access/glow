One form, one button. Your agent goes into the AHG 2026 collection with your name on it, and you get a reply within about a minute. No Git, no fork, no pull request.

Which agent are you sharing?

In VS Code, your agent is my-agent/SKILL.md in the kit folder. On the browser path, choose the SKILL.md you downloaded, or skip this and paste your agent into the box below.

Filled in for you when you choose the file, or by "Fill in the practice template". Or paste the agent your assistant gave you.

Lowercase words joined by hyphens, like alternate-format-planner. Filled in from the file. Not needed for a practice agent.

What happens on GitHub

  1. GitHub opens the "Share my agent" form, already filled in: the workshop code, which agent it is, and your agent.
  2. Move to the end of the form and select the privacy check box: your agent contains nothing private.
  3. Press Submit new issue.

Within about a minute, a reply appears on that page. Either your agent is in, with a link to it, or the reply says exactly what to fix: edit the issue, fix it, and save, and it is checked again straight away. Your agent is committed in your name, so it shows on your own GitHub profile.

If anything goes wrong

Raise a hand, or write to support@community-access.org. If you are stuck, share one of the ready-made agents (in the kit, examples/agents/) with your name on its author line. That counts.
